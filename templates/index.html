Frequently Asked Questions

The application supports CSV (.csv) and Excel (.xlsx, .xls) files. You can upload multiple files at once, and they will be merged automatically before cleaning.

Our algorithm analyzes your data and applies appropriate cleaning techniques based on the detected data types:

  • Text columns: Standardizes case, removes excess whitespace, and can normalize characters (remove accents)
  • Numeric columns: Extracts values from formatted strings (like "$1,234.56"), ensures proper numeric types
  • Date columns: Detects various date formats and converts them to standardized datetime
  • Contact details: Validates and standardizes email addresses and phone numbers
  • Boolean values: Converts various representations (Yes/No, True/False, 0/1) to standard boolean values

You can customize the cleaning process with these options:

  • Remove empty rows: Removes rows where all values are empty
  • Remove duplicates: Eliminates duplicate rows from the dataset
  • Standardize text: Converts text to consistent case format
  • Fill missing values: Intelligently fills nulls based on column type
  • Fix date formats: Standardizes various date formats
  • Trim whitespace: Removes leading/trailing spaces
  • Fix phone numbers: Standardizes phone number formats
  • Fix email addresses: Validates and standardizes emails
  • Fix boolean values: Standardizes yes/no, true/false representations
  • Extract numeric values: Extracts numbers from currency and other formatted text
  • Normalize text: Removes accents and special characters

Your data is processed securely and temporarily stored only for the duration of the cleaning process. Original uploads are deleted after processing, and only the cleaned output file is retained for download. We do not share, sell, or use your data content for any purpose other than providing the cleaning service.

Our application now features an advanced AI-powered recommendation system that analyzes your data and suggests the most appropriate cleaning operations:

  • Intelligent Analysis: When you upload a file, our AI (powered by Google's Gemini) analyzes your data structure, content, and potential issues
  • Personalized Recommendations: The AI generates tailored cleaning suggestions specific to your dataset
  • One-Click Application: You can apply all recommendations with a single click or select specific ones
  • Confidence Indicators: Each recommendation includes a confidence level so you know which suggestions are most important

The AI recommendations appear in a card above the cleaning options after you upload or select a sample file. For more details, see our AI Feature Documentation.
